Our Story

Built in Shenzhen, Shipping Worldwide

Haosheng (Shenzhen) Industrial Co., Ltd. (濠盛（深圳）实业有限公司) designs, manufactures and exports children's plastic toys under the HILLS TOYS brand. Based in Shenzhen, we operate within easy reach of the Shantou and Dongguan toy manufacturing belts — the deepest toy supply chain in the world.

We focus on four categories where we can truly add value: magnetic track systems, pretend-play beauty sets, educational fishing games and vehicle/figure collections. Each line is developed with export markets in mind — safe materials, retail-ready packaging and price points that leave room for your margin.

Our export team, led by sales manager Andy Su, works in English and Chinese and handles everything from first quotation to container loading — clear communication, honest lead times and documents that pass customs without drama.
