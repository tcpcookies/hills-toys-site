FAQ

Buyer Questions, Answered

What is your minimum order quantity?

MOQ starts from 3–10 cartons per item depending on the line. New customers can start with a trial order or a mixed container to test the market.

Can I put my own brand on the products?

Yes. We offer logo printing, custom color boxes, blister cards and full ODM development including new tooling. Most OEM orders carry no extra tooling cost above agreed quantities.

Are your toys safe for the US and EU markets?

Our materials comply with EN71 (EU), ASTM F963 and CPSIA (US). SGS or Intertek test reports can be arranged for each new item and season.

How do payments work?

Standard terms are T/T with 30% deposit and balance against B/L copy. L/C at sight is available for orders above USD 50,000. Alibaba trade assurance is also welcome.

What is the typical lead time?

25–35 days after sample approval and deposit. During peak season (August–November) please plan an extra 5–10 days.

Can you ship from other suppliers together with ours?

Absolutely. We regularly consolidate goods from multiple factories into one container and prepare a single set of export documents.

Do you provide free samples?

Stock samples are free — you only pay the courier cost. Customized samples are quoted based on printing or tooling work.

How can I get your latest catalog?

Email andy@hillstoys.cn or message us on WhatsApp — we will send the full catalog with FOB prices and packing details within 24 hours.
